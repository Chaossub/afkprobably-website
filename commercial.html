SHOP-WIDE COMMERCIAL RIGHTS

Sell the prints. Keep the files private.

Buy the AFKProbably meshes you want, then choose one commercial plan for permission to sell physical prints made from every mesh owned by your account.

What the commercial license covers

  • Commercial rights apply only to AFKProbably meshes purchased by the licensed account.
  • Monthly Commercial: 5% off AFKProbably digital files while the subscription is active.
  • Lifetime Commercial: 10% off AFKProbably digital files.
  • You may sell physical prints you manufacture from purchased meshes.
  • You may modify purchased meshes and continue selling physical prints made from those modified versions.
  • You may not redistribute, share, sell, sublicense, or upload the original or modified digital files, and you may not claim the original or modified AFKProbably design as your own.
  • The license does not unlock or include meshes you have not purchased.
  • Monthly Commercial automatically renews through Stripe each month until canceled. Canceling stops future renewals, but commercial rights stay active through the end of the current paid period.
  • Lifetime Commercial is a one-time purchase with no recurring charge and does not expire unless the license is terminated for a material violation.
