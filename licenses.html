Made for hobby printing and non-commercial use.

  • Print copies for yourself.

  • Modify the file for your own personal prints.
  • Do not sell physical prints made from the mesh.
  • Do not share, upload, trade, or resell the digital files.

COMMERCIAL

Commercial License

For makers who want to sell physical prints they produce.

  • Sell physical prints made from the purchased mesh.
  • Use your own photos and branding to market those physical prints.
  • No attribution is required unless a product listing says otherwise.
  • Do not sell, share, sublicense, or redistribute the digital mesh.
  • Do not upload the mesh to marketplaces, repositories, or print-on-demand services where others can access the file.
