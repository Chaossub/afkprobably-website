Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

AFKProbably (“AFKProbably,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use afkprobably.com, purchase digital products, request a commission, or contact us.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, commission details, reference links, messages, account information, marketing-email preferences, and information needed to provide purchased or commissioned files.

Payment information is processed by our payment provider. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number.

How we use information

  • Process purchases, licenses, and commission requests.
  • Communicate about orders, payments, commissions, and file delivery.
  • Send optional emails about new prints, limited drops, and shop updates when you choose to subscribe.
  • Provide access to purchased digital files and customer accounts.
  • Operate, protect, troubleshoot, and improve the website.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents.
  • Comply with legal and accounting obligations.

Service providers

We may share information with service providers that help operate the website and fulfill transactions, such as hosting, database/storage, payment processing, and transactional email providers. They receive information only as needed to provide those services.

Cookies and technical information

We may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies that are necessary for account sessions, security, checkout, and site functionality. We may also receive basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, and request logs.

Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain transaction and licensing records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and protect the website.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

Promotional emails are optional. You can change your new-print email preference from your account or use the unsubscribe link included in promotional messages. Unsubscribing from promotional emails does not stop necessary transactional messages such as receipts, commission updates, security notices, password resets, or purchased-file delivery.

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with you, request a correction, or request deletion where applicable. Some records may need to be retained for legal, fraud-prevention, licensing, or transaction-record purposes.

Children’s privacy

The website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Third-party links

Our website may link to third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or practices change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are posted.
